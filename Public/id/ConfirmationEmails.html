Confirmation Emails Sent to Clients

Every paid PrTask transaction triggers four confirmation emails to the cardholder's verified email address. Each email is timestamped, references the task ID and (where applicable) the Maya transaction reference, and links back to the on-platform record. Together these emails form the digital equivalent of an acknowledgment / delivery receipt trail. The four emails below are the production templates rendered with the same example transaction (PR cli/cli#1) used in the delivery receipt page.

Note for Maya Business reviewers: The email content below is the actual production template. In a live transaction every {placeholder} is replaced with the corresponding TaskEntity, PullRequestEntity, PaymentEntity, and UserEntity field from the platform database. Sender domain is prtask.com via SendGrid, with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records published.

How these emails fit into the delivery proof

The four emails above arrive in the cardholder's verified inbox over the lifetime of a single PrTask transaction. Together with the merged pull request on GitHub and the corresponding entry in the system event log, they form a complete, independently verifiable record that the digital service was delivered, accepted by the cardholder, and paid for. The Stage 3 email is the canonical delivery acknowledgment because it documents the cardholder's explicit accept-and-merge action; the Stage 4 email is the paid receipt linking that delivery to the Maya Business transaction reference.
